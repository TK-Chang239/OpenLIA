Economic season diagnostic

Identifies the current macro quadrant (spring / summer / autumn / winter) and maps it to asset class positioning. Run monthly when PMI and CPI data refresh.

Section A — quadrant inputs
Growth signal PMI trend · GDP qoq · direction
Inflation signal CPI yoy · core · direction
Credit conditions IG / HY spreads vs 12m avg
Real rate trend TIPS 10yr: rising / flat / falling
Section B — season verdict
Primary season e.g. Autumn — slowing growth + sticky inflation
Confidence level clear / mixed / transitioning
Nearest comparable period e.g. 2022 H1, 1973–74
Transition risk which adjacent season could emerge and why
Section C — asset playbook for current season
Tailwind assets best performers in this quadrant historically
Headwind assets historically weak in this quadrant
Portfolio stress test if current season persists 2–4 quarters, what breaks?
